# Design System: Desanara

## Overview

**Creative north star: "Pasar desa yang rapi".** The public site should feel like any good marketplace the buyer already
knows (search first, photo grid, price, one obvious button) while the village is always visible: every card names its
village, every village has a page that reads like a destination. Friendly, light, quick on a cheap Android phone.
Replaces the earlier "Buku Nota & Stempel" concept (rejected 2026-11-03: too metaphorical, felt like a form, not a shop).

## Colors

- **Desanara green** `#387b4c`: platform accent (primary buttons, links, search submit), the dark end of the logo
  gradient. White on it 5.1:1. Inside a village page the accent becomes that village's own colour (ADR 0007).
- **Secondary palette** (tokens `sprout`, `leaf`, `sun`, `clay`): decoration, illustration and highlights only.
  `sprout` `#9dd950` (the logo's light leaf tip) and `sun` `#e3a84f` take dark text (10.4:1 and 8.3:1); `leaf`
  `#6cac4e` (the wordmark green, 2.8:1) and `clay` `#c2612c` (4.2:1 with white) only for large text (≥ 19 px bold) or
  non-text shapes.
- **WhatsApp green** `#0f7a3f`: only for ordering/chat actions, in every village, so the action is always recognised.
- **Neutrals**: white surfaces (cards), warm cream `wash` `#f9f4e7` backgrounds for sections and tiles, `line` borders.
  Text is warm near-black.
- **Status**: green = open, amber = temporarily closed, neutral = closed. Verified uses the accent-soft tint.
- Village page covers can use a gradient when they have no photo. The mobile catalogue banner uses a dark scrim to
  keep text legible over the illustration. Village accents keep 3:1 contrast
  on white (enforced in code) and are used for headings, icons and badges, not for body text.

## Logo

Leaf "d" mark plus a Montserrat wordmark (outlined). Vector masters in `docs/brand/desanara/` (one path per letter
and leaf, ids for motion), generated by `docs/brand/build-logo.py`; served copies in `apps/web/public/brand/`. The mark is
tall, so the horizontal logo needs at least 32 px height (header 44 px). Use the `Logo` / `LogoMark` components; never re-type the wordmark in a font. Rules: `docs/brand/README.md`.

## Typography

Montserrat for platform pages and the dashboard (variable, 400–800), the same family as the logo wordmark.
Public page titles use `page-title` (20 px on phones, 24 px from `sm`). Section titles use `section-title`
(18 px on phones, 20 px from `sm`). The home banner uses 20–22 px on smaller screens and `display-title`
from `md`. The about hero uses `display-title` (24–36 px).
Body text stays at least 16 px with a line length of up to 68ch. Search hints and compact controls use 14 px,
and supporting labels use 12–14 px. Search input text stays 16 px on phones. Keep card names to two lines
and check long names at 320 px. Villages may swap the display face through `font-village` for their own
headings. Prices use bold, tabular numerals.

## Layout & shape

Container `max-w-6xl`, 16 px gutters on phones. Product grids: 2 columns on phones, 3 on tablets, 4 on desktop.
Cards, panels, and stand-alone photo surfaces use `rounded-(--radius)` (12 px by default). Village themes
can override this token. Thumbnails inside cards and compact rectangular controls use
`rounded-(--radius-compact)` (8 px).
Prominent actions, the search field, chips, and badges use pill corners. The filter sheet shares the card
radius on its top edge, and the story viewer stays flush with the screen on phones. Use HeroUI v3 for form controls,
buttons and overlays. Keep filters and toolbar controls compact, with 36 px fields and secondary actions,
40 px search, and at least 44 px for the main order action and bottom navigation targets.

## Components

- **Header** uses a sticky logo, a neutral search pill, and a thin divider over a translucent surface with backdrop blur. On mobile, the drawer holds Tentang and
  Daftarkan UMKM. Desktop shows Beranda, Produk, Desa, Tentang, and Pengaturan beside the search field. Wide desktops
  also show Daftarkan UMKM.
- **Mobile navigation** puts Beranda, Produk, Desa, and Pengaturan in a fixed translucent bar with backdrop blur.
  The bar adds 8 px of space below the tabs and accounts for the device safe area. Each destination has an
  icon and a label. The current destination and hovered tabs use green text and a tint behind the icon.
- **Settings** shows the display language choices with an explicit selected state. The page is available in both locales.
- **Catalogue home** opens with search, available stories, and the newest products. On phones the first two
  products share a row, with a link to the full catalogue. Categories follow in a horizontal row of tinted icons
  with labels. The promotion slot follows categories, then the village cards. Sponsored campaigns name their
  sponsor. Multiple campaigns use swipe and previous/next controls. The village discovery banner fills an empty
  slot. Desktop shows four products and a grid of category links. Keep the home village preview to six cards. The village directory shows 12 cards per
  page, with equal horizontal padding around the home directory link's hover area.
  The seller invitation uses a white card and the HeroUI primary green action.
- **Catalogue results** retain the current filters when searching and use compact category links. A full-width
  HeroUI bottom `Sheet` groups region, village, category, and sorting in list and map modes. Region, village,
  and category use searchable HeroUI `Autocomplete` fields. Regional selection narrows the village options.
  Field values sit vertically centred inside consistent 36 px triggers. The form stays centred at a readable
  width on desktop, and the trigger shows the number of active selections. Phone results use rows with the
  photo beside the title, village, and price. Desktop uses the product grid.
  Loading placeholders follow the result layout. A white status card gives a relevant next action for an empty
  search or catalogue. A failed load offers a retry with the current query intact. Partial map failures leave
  available pins and list navigation accessible.
- **Status illustrations** use Nara, the rice-sprout mascot, in the public site and dashboard. Both apps use the
  same page and notice layouts, shape tokens, and art. Show the illustration next to the message and action, with
  a smaller size in cards. Inline status messages keep their footprint small where useful content remains visible.
  See the [mascot guide](docs/brand/mascot.md) for poses and assets.
- **Data feedback** keeps loading, empty, unavailable, access-restricted, and unexpected error states distinct.
  Use a skeleton shaped like the content for an initial load. Keep previously loaded content and draft fields
  visible during a temporary failure, with a clear retry at the affected section. Hide protected cached data
  when a read confirms that the session, access, or record is gone. Both apps show an offline notice.
  A placeholder uses the content's grid, row height, image ratio and breakpoints. Keep ready navigation and
  headings visible. Reserve space only for the section that is still loading.
- **Catalogue map** shares the catalogue search and filters. Production uses public village centre coordinates.
  Compact circles show the number of matching shop locations up to `99+` on a pannable Leaflet basemap,
  with 44 px touch targets. Villages without shop locations use a pin icon. The details panel shows
  the full village name and total matching businesses. The map fills the available viewport
  below a compact toolbar. On mobile, its own controls replace the global header and bottom navigation. A labelled
  list link above the provider credit returns to the results with the same search and filters. Village results
  open in a bottom sheet with a drag handle. Swiping down, tapping outside, or pressing Escape dismisses the
  sheet and returns to the map. It lists every matching business with compact
  type and caps business names at two lines. Three or more cards use a horizontal swipe rail with previous
  and next controls. Tapping a village group zooms to the first scale that separates
  its pins. Selecting a village hides its count marker and fits the map to the shop coordinates available to
  the public. If the selected village has no shop coordinates available, a village-centre pin keeps the map
  oriented. The list includes shops without public coordinates. Catalogue preview uses fictional storefront
  points marked as sample data. Pinch, mouse wheel, and trackpad gestures control the map within its canvas.
- **Story preview** puts a horizontally scrollable row of portrait photo cards below search, before products.
  Each card belongs to one seller and shows the newest update as its cover, with a compact 11 px name.
  A green dot marks unread updates and a check marks a completed set. Unread sellers come first, with
  both groups ordered by their latest update. Viewed status is kept in the current browser.
  The viewer supports photos, portrait clips, and text on a solid green background. Clips start muted and
  offer a sound toggle beside the caption. Each update gets one
  progress segment. Photos and text play for six seconds. Video progress follows playback. Story cards can show
  a compact pre-order or shop-open indicator when availability is known. The prototype exercises both indicators
  in catalogue preview mode. A linked product card shows the published name and price when available.
  Playback continues to the next seller, then closes
  after the final update. Viewers can pause and resume, hold the photo, swipe or drag it horizontally,
  or use the arrows. On devices with a mouse, the arrows appear on viewer hover or when they receive focus.
  Playback pauses when the page is hidden. Reopening a card starts at the first unread update. Desktop uses a
  centred portrait viewer.
- **About hero** (splash-style, from the brand board mockup): on phones the vertical logo, the village landscape
  (`VillageScene`, inline SVG in the brand palette, one rise-on-load motion, none with reduced motion), headline
  "Karya Desa, untuk Indonesia." in the accent, then full-width "Jelajahi Sekarang" (primary) and "Daftarkan UMKM"
  (outline). From `md`: text left, landscape right with edges faded into the cream by a mask.
- **Search form** uses a HeroUI `ComboBox` with a pill-shaped `InputGroup`. After two characters, a compact
  list under the field links to matching products and businesses. A free-text search submits a GET request
  with the current filters. HeroUI draws one focus ring around the group.
- **Compact actions** use short labels that fit on one line at 320 px in both locales. For example, the
  floating map action reads “Daftar” or “List” and its accessible label describes the destination.
- **Product card** shows a photo or tinted category tile, product name (two lines), village of origin, and tabular
  price. Mobile homepage images use a 4:3 ratio to keep prices close to the top of the catalogue. The homepage uses
  green prices and keeps seller information on the detail page. Out-of-stock uses a
  dark pill on the photo. The entire card opens the detail page.
- **Village card / village page**: village links on the catalogue home show the crest, name, area, and business count.
  Village pages show a cover photo or a gradient in the village colour, a white-framed crest, village display font and
  the verification badge. Official crests are shown untouched.
  A thin circular border frames the crest. A labelled regency emblem may fill the slot until a village logo is available.
- **Product detail** on phones uses a compact title bar above the gallery. The name and price lead into two linked
  rows for the village and seller.
- **Product gallery** supports swipe, arrow buttons, and keyboard navigation. Tapping a photo opens it uncropped in a
  dark full-screen viewer. When there are multiple photos, the viewer shows the current position and a thumbnail strip.
- **Order panel (product page)** puts the WhatsApp-green button after the origin and seller. The quantity stepper
  and note sit in an expandable section. The total updates when the quantity exceeds one.
- **Open-status badge**: dot + label (Buka / Tutup / Tutup sementara) with detail text where there is room; never shown
  without a schedule.
- **Verified badge**: accent-soft pill with a check icon.
- **Footer** uses a white surface and a thin border. It carries the platform description, links, and credits
  with the monochrome HIMASIF and SATU University logos.

## Do

- Put the village name on every product surface.
- Keep one primary action per screen; ordering is always WhatsApp green.
- Design the no-photo state as a normal state: most products start without photos.
  Keep the photo frame and center a size-limited category icon on a soft tinted surface.
- Test at 360 px width.

## Don't

- No skeuomorphic or metaphor-heavy styling (paper, stamps, receipts).
- No on-screen "demo" labels (sample data is controlled through `is_seed` and `db:seed:reset`, see PRODUCT.md).
- No custom CSS/HTML from villages; only tokens (ADR 0007).
- Don't redraw partner or village logos.
